## 1. Purpose

Make sure peer work hours and mileage are recorded accurately, approved promptly, and paid correctly, with records that can support audits and regulatory review.

## 2. Policy

- All peer work time and work-related driving is logged in the **SHLC Time Entries** list (Microsoft Lists).
- Mileage is reimbursed **only to the driver** using a personal vehicle for SHLC business.
- Mileage is paid at the **standard route distance** in the Routes list. Odometer readings are used only for destinations not yet on the list.
- Commuting between home and the normal work site is **not** reimbursable.
- **Resident names are never entered.** Use the resident ID or initials only.
- Entries must be submitted **by the end of the next business day**.
- Falsifying time or mileage is grounds for discipline up to and including termination.

## 3. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Peer | Log each work block and trip accurately and on time. Correct returned entries within 1 business day. |
| Supervisor | Approve, return, or deny entries within **2 business days**. Review every flag before approving. |
| Tracker Admin | Maintain Locations, Routes, Staff, Settings. Process location requests within 3 business days. |
| Payroll | Process the biweekly export. Report discrepancies to the Compliance Officer. |
| Compliance Officer | System owner. Monthly audit. Investigates flagged patterns. |

## 4. Procedure — Peers

1. Open the **Microsoft Lists** app → **Time Entries** → **+ New**.
2. Enter **Work Date**, **Clock In**, **Clock Out**.
3. Select **Activity Type**.
4. Select **Start Location** and **End Location** from the list.
   - Not on the list? Select **Other**, enter **odometer start and end**, and submit a **Location Request**.
5. Set **Round Trip** (Yes if you returned to the start location).
6. Set **Drove Personal Vehicle** (Yes only if you were the driver).
7. **Resident Ref:** ID or initials only.
8. **Purpose:** one or two factual sentences (e.g., "Transported resident to scheduled medical appointment.").
9. Set **Status = Submitted** and save.
10. If you get a **Returned** email, fix the entry and set Status = Submitted again.

## 5. Procedure — Supervisors

1. Open the approval in **Teams → Approvals** or the email.
2. Review hours, locations, miles, purpose, and **any flags**.
3. Choose:
   - **Approve** — entry is accurate.
   - **Return for correction** — fixable error; state exactly what to fix.
   - **Deny** — not work-related or not reimbursable; state the reason. Compliance is copied automatically.
4. Flagged entries: confirm the reason before approving and note it in comments.

## 6. Flags and what they mean

| Flag | Supervisor action |
|---|---|
| Shift over limit | Confirm the shift was authorized |
| Trip over mileage limit | Confirm destination and route |
| Non-standard location | Confirm destination; make sure a Location Request was submitted |
| No standard route – odometer used | Check odometer readings are reasonable |
| Overlaps another entry | Return for correction unless a legitimate split |

## 7. Payroll

- Export runs automatically every other Monday at 6:00 AM.
- Only **Approved** entries in the pay period are paid.
- Entries approved after the cutoff roll to the next pay period.

## 8. Audit & Compliance

- **Monthly:** Compliance reviews the Flagged view and a random sample of 10 entries for accuracy and resident-identifier compliance.
- **Annually:** Tracker Admin re-verifies all Routes mileage.
- Records are retained per SHLC record retention policy.
